Font audit — rl-0l1

Two families are loaded: Cormorant Garamond (the display serif — always italic, always light: the welcome title, the field-guide title, the park name in the strip) and Space Mono (everything else). Tate does not love the cursive-looking serif. Each card below renders the app's real strings at the app's real sizes on the app's real palette, so the comparison is faithful. No site code changes until a pick is made.

Section A — the display serif

The mockups: a modal title ("Resonant Landscapes", 48 px italic light) and the park strip (mint band, park name at 22 px italic light). The serif appears in the app only in these display moments — no body text — so legibility at small size matters less than character at large size.

Section B — Space Mono, for completeness

The complaint named the cursive one, and Space Mono is the other half of every screen: all tips, labels, buttons and captions. Two alternatives, same mockups: a field-guide tip at 12 px, an uppercase label at 11 px with 0.16em tracking, and the install button.

Constraints carried over

Every candidate is on Google Fonts (so the non-blocking load, the preconnects and the swap strategy in index.html carry over unchanged); every candidate ships woff2, so the service worker's globPatterns precache picks it up with no change; all support iOS 15/Safari 15; none touch zoom behaviour (WCAG 1.4.4). The one thing a swap does change is the fallback stack in tailwind.config and — for Fraunces and Newsreader — the font request URL gains variable axes, which is a slightly heavier request than Cormorant's static weights. Nothing here is a reason not to swap; it is what the swap PR will carry.
